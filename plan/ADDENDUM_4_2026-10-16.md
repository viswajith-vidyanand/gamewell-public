# Addendum 4 to Analysis Plan v1 (2026-10-16)
Recorded before any sensitivity analysis is run. Plan v1 and earlier addenda are unchanged.

1. Primary result and diagnostics (Addendum 3). The diagnostic refit reproduced the original fixed effects (max absolute difference 1.3e-15); two primary fits have been run in total. The primary fit is singular (random-intercept SD 0, ICC 0), residual SD 12.44, residual skewness -0.75, excess kurtosis 3.51, and residual spread declines from about 15-16 in lower fitted-value bins to 4.6 in the top decile, consistent with ceiling compression. Primary results are unchanged: b1 = 0.0731 valence points per hour, model 95% CI -0.135 to 0.281, CR2 95% CI -0.143 to 0.289, category (a). These diagnostics do not change the model; they are carried as limitations, and the cluster-robust interval remains part of the wider-interval rule.

2. Reporting clarification for exposure_between. Because prior_between (the participant's mean prior valence) closely tracks the participant's mean later valence, exposure_between is conditional on a near-copy of the person-level outcome. It is therefore not an unadjusted between-person association and must not be described as one. It is reported with this caveat and is not a confirmatory result.

3. Operational definitions (same formula, REML, default optimizer, one fit each; derived variables recomputed within each cohort; model-based Satterthwaite and CR2 95% CIs, wider interval used; category assigned by code with SESOI +/-0.5 valence points per hour of exposure_within):
 7a: maximum interval 30h, 36h, 72h, and no cutoff (four cohorts; valence present at both endpoints, linked, exposure non-missing).
 7b: (i) exposure = log(1 + minutes); estimate and CIs expressed per 1 SD of within-person transformed exposure and compared with +/-0.8326 valence points per SD (the SESOI of 0.5 per hour times the primary-cohort pooled within-person exposure SD of 1.6651 hours); (ii) exposure in hours capped at the 99th percentile of primary-cohort exposure hours (cap value recorded), judged against +/-0.5 per hour.
 7c: any-play indicator (minutes > 0); within = indicator minus the participant's proportion of play intervals; estimate per 1 unit of within-person change and per 1 SD; descriptive, with no category assigned and outside the robustness label.
 7d: Xbox-only-linked and Nintendo-only-linked subsets of the primary cohort; a subset is fitted only if it has at least 100 participants with at least 2 eligible intervals, otherwise counts only; the both-linked group is reported as counts only if below 100. Outside the robustness label.
 7e: exclude participants with fewer than 5 eligible intervals in the primary cohort.
 7f: exclude intervals in which prior or later valence equals 0.
 7g: add a random slope for exposure_within, (1 + exposure_within | participant). Report the model-based CI, and the CR2 CI if clubSandwich supports the fitted object; otherwise the category is assigned from the model-based CI, and this is stated.
 7h: category assigned from the CR2 CI alone, using the primary-model results; no new fit.
 7i: leave-one-participant-out refits (371 fits); report the distribution of the exposure_within estimate (minimum, 2.5th percentile, median, 97.5th percentile, maximum) and the number of refits whose category (model-based CI) differs from the primary category. No participant identifiers are saved or printed. Outside the robustness label.
