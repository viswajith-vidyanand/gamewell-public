# Addendum 2 to Analysis Plan v1 (2026-10-16)
Recorded after the blinded power simulation and BEFORE any model containing both real later valence and exposure is fitted. Plan v1 is unchanged.

1. Blinded power simulation (analysis/gamewell_power_simulation_2026-10-15/, v2 outputs). Primary cohort: 5,076 intervals, 371 participants, 171 with exposure variation. Estimated 80% minimum detectable within-person effect: 0.354 valence points per hour of recorded play (0.029 within-person valence SD per hour; 0.589 points per 1 SD of within-person exposure). Power was 5.4% at a true effect of 0, 66.0% at 0.25 points per hour, 99.8% at 0.5, and about 100% at 1 or more. The outcome-only model estimated the random-intercept SD at zero (singular), and singular fits occurred in 47.6% to 56.0% of simulated fits. The simulation assumes independent normal residuals, so the minimum detectable effect is a best-case figure.

2. Smallest effect size of interest (SESOI): plus or minus 0.5 valence points per hour of additional within-person recorded linked-console play. This is a judgment-based threshold for a small but not trivial association. It was chosen after viewing the simulation summary and before any real exposure-outcome estimate exists. It applies equally to the secondary life-satisfaction analysis, which remains labelled secondary.

3. Interpretation rule for b1, using the 95% interval defined in item 4:
(a) interval entirely within +/-0.5: no association larger than the SESOI is supported;
(b) interval excludes 0 and extends beyond +/-0.5: association of potentially meaningful size;
(c) interval excludes 0 but lies entirely within +/-0.5: statistically detectable but small;
(d) interval includes 0 and extends beyond +/-0.5: inconclusive.
All wording remains associational and applies only to recorded linked-console play.

4. Interval used for interpretation: the wider of the model-based (Satterthwaite) 95% CI and the cluster-robust (CR2, clubSandwich, clustered by participant) 95% CI for b1. Both are reported. If the primary fit is singular, the estimate of b1 from the Plan v1 model is still the primary estimate, the singular status is reported, and nothing about the model is changed in response.

5. Robustness label: a result is described as robust only if it falls in the same category under item 3 in sensitivity analyses 7a, 7b, 7e, 7f, 7g and 7h of Plan v1. Otherwise it is reported as sensitive to the stated analytic choice. Sensitivity analyses 7c, 7d and 7i are reported but do not enter this label.

6. The primary model is fitted once on the Plan v1 cohort. Any later change is recorded in a further dated addendum. Outputs are aggregate only.
