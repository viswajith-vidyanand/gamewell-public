# Addendum 1 to Analysis Plan v1 (clarifications only; Plan v1 is unchanged)
1. Within-person SD of valence, used to scale the primary estimate in Plan section 5, is defined as the pooled SD of (later valence minus that participant's own mean later valence) across intervals in the primary cohort. The scaled effect is b1 divided by this SD, reported alongside b1 in valence points per hour.
2. The primary model is fitted with lme4 (via lmerTest for inference) in R. Software versions are recorded in ENVIRONMENT_SESSIONINFO.txt.
3. All centring and person-mean quantities in Plan section 3 are computed within the primary cohort as defined in Plan section 2, and recomputed within each sensitivity cohort.
4. The smallest effect of interest and minimum detectable effect will be recorded in Addendum 2, before the primary model is fitted.
