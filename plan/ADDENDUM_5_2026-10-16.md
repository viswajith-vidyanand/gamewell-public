# Addendum 5 to Analysis Plan v1 (2026-10-16)
1. Sensitivity analyses (Addendum 4) were completed. By the Addendum 4 item 4 rule the primary valence result is labelled robust: all required analyses (7a four cohorts, 7b both versions, 7e, 7f, 7g, 7h) fell in category (a). Disclosure: an initial run of 7a applied the 48-hour primary limit by error; four invalid 7a fits were executed, preserved, and not used, and four corrected 7a fits under the Addendum 4 definition are the reported results.
2. Clarification on 7b(i): the comparison threshold of +/-0.8326 per SD converts the SESOI using the primary-cohort pooled within-person SD of exposure in hours (1.6651), whereas the estimate is per SD of log(1 + minutes). This is an approximate standardization and is reported as such. Analyses 7c, 7d and 7i remain outside the robustness label.
3. Secondary life-satisfaction analysis (Plan v1 section 6; separate construct, "I was satisfied with my life today"). Cohort: linked Xbox and/or Nintendo; interval <= 48 hours; life satisfaction present at both endpoints; exposure non-missing. Model: the Plan v1 section 4 formula with later life satisfaction as the outcome, and prior life satisfaction decomposed into prior_between and prior_within; exposure and interval-length variables recomputed within this cohort; REML, default optimizer, random intercept for participant. Inference: model-based (Satterthwaite) and CR2 95% CIs; the wider interval is used; category (a/b/c/d) assigned by code using the Addendum 2 item 3 rule with SESOI +/-0.5 points per hour (Addendum 2 item 2). Within-person life-satisfaction SD is defined as the pooled SD of (later life satisfaction minus the participant's own mean later life satisfaction) in this cohort.
4. Exactly two fits are run: Fit 1 on the full life-satisfaction cohort; Fit 2 after excluding intervals in which prior or later life satisfaction equals 0. The analysis is labelled secondary; no robustness label is assigned; no combined well-being score is created; life satisfaction is never merged with valence in any model or summary.
5. The Addendum 4 item 2 caveat applies to exposure_between in these models: it is conditional on prior_between and is not an unadjusted between-person association.
6. No power simulation was run for life satisfaction. Results are interpreted by the pre-set rule and the width of the reported interval.
